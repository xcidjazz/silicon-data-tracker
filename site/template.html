01 — Price

GPU rental price vs token expenditure index

Rental prices on the left axis in dollars per GPU-hour; the token expenditure index on the right in dollars per million tokens. Raw levels, no rebasing. Token indices share one hue in three shades; each GPU has its own hue, with hyperscaler lighter and dashed against neo-cloud. L and R in the legend mark which axis a series is read against.

From 22 Aug, lengthening daily. Silicon Data publishes a rolling 7-day window with no backfill, so the record starts the day the scrape started — Citadel licence the history, ours accrues. With two scales the visual gap between the lines is arbitrary: only the shape of each line, and where it turns, carries information.

Rental term structure and forward curve

All three GPUs out to 36 months. Each GPU is a hue; the term rate is solid, the forward rate dashed and lighter. Tenor 0 is spot, where term and forward meet by construction. Switch to Compare vintages to overlay today's curve against a week, a month and six months ago for one GPU.

The public site only plots 0–6 months. The 36-month point — the one used to talk about backwardation — is in the payload but never shown on screen.

03 — Spend per employee

Spend per employee, overall distribution

Monthly AI spend per employee across the distribution. Plotted on a log axis — the top 1% and the median differ by roughly 600×, and on a linear axis the median would be pinned flat to the floor.

Naming trap. The figure usually quoted as the “90th percentile” is Top 10% median, not P90 — 650 ÷ 11.95 = 54×, the widely cited ratio. P90 ÷ median is only 22×. Both are on this chart; they are not interchangeable.
